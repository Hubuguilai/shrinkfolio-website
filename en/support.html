How can we help?

A practical guide to Shrinkfolio.

Compress a photo

  1. Choose an image in Photos or Files.
  2. Adjust quality, output format and scale.
  3. Compress, review the result, then save or share the copy.

Pro and restoring a purchase

Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks batch compression, target file size and maximum pixel dimensions. To restore, open the Pro screen and tap Restore using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Why is the file larger or above my target?

Results depend on the original, format and settings. Try another format, a higher target, or allow smaller dimensions. Some targets cannot be reached.

Photos, formats and recovery

JPEG replaces transparency with white; Live Photos and animations produce a still image. HDR and metadata may not be fully retained. Recovery after an interruption is best effort: check Photos before saving again if the save status is uncertain.
