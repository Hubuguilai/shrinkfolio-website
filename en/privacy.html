Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Photos and files

Shrinkfolio processes selected images on your device; it does not send your photos to a Shrinkfolio server. Apple Photos may download an iCloud item to provide it to the app. Photos permission is used to select or save images; adding copies to original albums requires broader access. You can change access in device settings.

Temporary files and recovery

The app creates temporary input and output copies for compression, previews and sharing. It also stores names, photo identifiers, settings and processing state locally to attempt to restore interrupted work. Clearing the list attempts to remove current session files. On startup the app attempts to remove matching temporary image copies last modified at least seven days earlier; recovery records older than seven days are removed when read. Interrupted share-extension work may leave temporary copies. The system can remove temporary files earlier; deletion is not secure erasure.

Metadata and sharing

Depending on your settings and image format, a copy may retain photo metadata, including location. If you choose to share a copy, the receiving app or service handles it under its own privacy terms.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases and payment information using StoreKit. The app checks Apple-provided purchase status to unlock and restore Pro. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may process transaction information under its privacy policy.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you provide, and use them to respond and address your request. Please avoid sending sensitive images or details unless needed. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement. We do not use advertising or analytics tools on this site.
